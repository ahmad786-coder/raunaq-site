At Raunaq ("we", "our", "us"), we treat the privacy, dignity, and modesty of every wedding host and guest as sacred. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, protect, and handle data when you use our host mobile application and guest web portals.

1. Information We Collect

  • Host Account Information: When you create a host account, we collect your email address, created timestamp, login activity timestamps, and policy acceptance records.
  • Event Media: Photos, videos, and audio wishes uploaded by wedding guests to your specific event.
  • Payment Claim Information: Bank transfer method, sender mobile number, sender account title, transaction reference ID, and screenshot proof voluntarily submitted for human verification. We never process, see, or store credit/debit card numbers or bank account passwords.

2. Guest Permissions & Download Protection

  • Upload-Only Model: Guests access an event via an event-specific QR code or web link. Guests can only submit photos, videos, and voice notes.
  • Zero Guest Browsing: Guests can NEVER view, browse, list, or download other guests' media or access the host dashboard.
  • Downloads are Host-Only: Full-resolution downloads and consolidated event ZIP packages are strictly restricted to the authenticated host account.

3. Purdah & Modesty Guarantee

  • Approval Mode (Purdah Guarantee): Hosts can enable approval-before-display for any event. When active, guest uploads remain hidden until individually reviewed and approved by the host.
  • Private Functions (Ladies-Only): Functions marked as "Private" (e.g. Mayun, Ladies Mehndi) are strictly excluded from the Live Wall and projector displays.
  • Immediate Hide Control: Any item marked hidden by the host is instantly removed from all display surfaces.

4. Data Retention & Lifecycle

  • Retention Period: Event media is hosted securely for six (6) months from the wedding date (or creation date).
  • Free ZIP Backup: Before any scheduled lifecycle purge, the host can download a consolidated ZIP package of all original high-resolution media.
  • 14-Day Grace Window: A 14-day grace period is provided following expiry before automated background deletion occurs.
  • Account Deletion Retention: Upon host request to delete their account, a 14-day recovery window is granted. After 14 days, all media files and event rows are permanently purged from Backblaze B2 and our database. Verified payment records are anonymised (amount, package, date, and partner commission retained; host email and identifiers permanently scrubbed).

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

  • No Data Selling: We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data or wedding memories to third-party advertisers or data brokers.
  • Infrastructure Providers: Media is stored in secure encrypted cloud storage (Backblaze B2) and database services (Supabase/PostgreSQL) strictly for service delivery.
