01 — How It Works

Effortless capture for مہمان and host

No logins, no App Store downloads, no WhatsApp group quality compression. Three seamless moments from venue table to permanent family archive.

01

Scan Table QR Card

Guests point any phone camera at elegant table cards. The private event portal opens instantly in Chrome or Safari without account creation or installs.

02

Upload Photos, Videos & Wishes

Pick photos, 4K videos, or iPhone HEICs right from the camera roll, or record a warm 30-second audio voice note wish. Uploads flow directly to secure cloud storage.

Live Wall & Master ZIP Archive

Approved memories project live on venue banquet screens during the reception. The host downloads every raw, uncompressed original file in one clean ZIP archive.

02 — Sacred Trust

The پردہ Mode Guarantee

Traditional values meet modern technology. We designed Raunaq so families never have to compromise modesty or peace of mind to enjoy modern digital celebrations.

01 / Four Guarantees

Approval-Before-Display Moderation

Every single guest upload lands in a private host moderation queue first. Nothing is visible in public galleries or projected on venue screens until approved by the family.

02 / Four Guarantees

Private Ladies Functions

Designate exclusive ladies-only functions with one tap. Media tagged to private functions is strictly restricted to the host app and physically excluded from the Live Wall.

03 / Four Guarantees

One-Tap Re-filing & Rescue

Accidentally misfiled photos can be moved immediately from public functions to private ladies functions right from the host gallery with zero page reloads.

04 / Four Guarantees

No Guest-Side Browsing

Guests only see an upload terminal and personal delivery receipts. Guests can never browse, view, download, or screenshot anyone else’s family photos from their browser.
